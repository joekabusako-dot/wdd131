KIJ Mobility - Plannig Document

Site Name: KIJ Mobility

This name represents a company that focus on transportation in Kinshasa with its Founder KIJ=Kabusako Iviti Joe

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to introduce a transportation service that will allow people to request transportation with Kinshasa and eventually, travel between Kinshasa and other cities and regions of the DR Congo.

Scenarios

  • How can I find a reliable transportation service ?
  • How much does transportation cost ?
  • Do I need car or motorcycle to go outside Kinshasa ?

Color Schema

  • white: Body background and heading and footer texts
  • Dark Red: Header and Footer backgrounds
  • Dark Yellow: other heading backgrounds
  • Black: paragraphs

Typography

  • Roboto: paragraphs, buttons & general texts
  • Monserrat: Headings
