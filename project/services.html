Transportation Services

KIJ Mobility provides convenient transportation solutions designed to meet the everyday mobility needs of people in Kinshasa.

Motorcycle Transportation

Our first transportation service is motorcycle transportation within Kinshasa. It is designed to provide a convenient and reliable option for people traveling around the city.

Why Choose Motorcycle Transportation?

  • Convenient for daily trips
  • Suitable for navigating busy city roads
  • Designed for reliable transportation within Kinshasa

How It Works

  1. Submit a ride request.
  2. Provide your pickup location and destination.
  3. Choose your transportation option.
  4. Receive information about your ride.
    5. Request a Ride
