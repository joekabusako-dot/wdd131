Who We Are

KIJ Mobility is a transportaton service based in Kinshasa, DR Congo. We are starting with motorcycle transportation to provide convenient and reliable mobility for people traveling within the city.

Our Story

KIJ Mobility was created with the goal of developing a reliable transportation service adapted to the mobility needs of people in the Democratic Republic of Congo. The business will begin with motorcycle transportation in Kinshasa and grow gradually as the service develops.

Convenience

We understand the importance of convenience in transportation. Our service is designed to make it easy for you to get where you need to go, whether it's for work, school, or leisure.

Reliability

Reliability is at the core of our service. We strive to provide consistent and dependable transportation options, ensuring that you can count on us to get you to your destination safely and on time.

Safety

Your safety is our top priority. We adhere to strict safety protocols and maintain our vehicles to the highest standards, so you can travel with peace of mind.

Our Mission

At KIJ Mobility, we are committed to providing safe, convenient, and reliable transportation services that meet the needs of our customers in Kinshasa and across DR Congo.

Our Vision

Our vision is to build a transportation network that connects people and communities across the Democratic Republic of Congo.

Motorcycle transportation will be our initial offering, with plans to expand the service as we grow and meet the needs of our community.

Car transportation will be added in the future as we expand our network.

Intercity transportation will be the next step in our expansion plans.

Expansion to other cities/regions in DR Congo is also part of our long-term vision.

Why KIJ Mobility

Transportation is an important part of everyday life in Kinshasa. KIJ Mobility aims to provide an accessible transportation option for people who need to move around the city efficiently.
